WSPR Logger
Live map options
MUF card range
Typical day over
Reporter arcs: a great-circle line to each station that heard the beacon in the last 60 minutes, stronger signals brighter and thicker; tap a station for its details. Day / Night: the night side and the grey line (dashed), redrawn every minute. Solar card: the space weather of the Now view (SFI, K and A index, X-ray, Bz, solar wind, aurora, proton flux, foF2, Juliusruh MUF) over the map, in the same colours. MUF card: a small MUF D=3000 graph (Juliusruh, the line) with the reporters of each transmission (the bars), as in the MUF view, over the MUF card range: the last 24 hours, 2 or 3 days or a week; foF2 on MUF card adds the Juliusruh foF2 as an amber line on the same MHz scale (stored from November 2026). Typical day card: the Typical day of the Now view in small — the stations heard in 60 minutes at each UTC hour, usually (the median over the chosen days, blue, with the middle half shaded) and today (orange); the corner compares this hour with the usual. The period is shared with the Now view. Follow beacon: the map moves with each new fix. Remember position: the map opens where you left it (the zoom is always remembered); off, it opens at the beacon. ⌂ on the map returns to the beacon. Refreshed every minute. Remembered on the phone.
Now options
Spots of
Typical day over
Propagation: the number of stations that heard the last transmission — Very low (≤ 5), Low (≤ 10), Normal (≤ 20), Good (≤ 40), Very good (≤ 60), Extremely good (≤ 100), Exceptional; & DX when two or more are beyond 6000 km, HIGH MUF when the Juliusruh MUF is above 28 MHz. Typical day: the stations heard in 60 minutes at each UTC hour — the median (blue line) and the middle half of the days (shaded) over the chosen period — with today in orange. Compared with history: the Analysis page's Right now card (the last 90 days). The colours of the solar values are those of the full page's Solar panel.

Distribution of
Sections
The stations (callsign and grid) that heard the beacon in the last 60 minutes, as the full page's Reporter list. The search matches the callsign, grid and country anywhere. Remembered on the phone.
MUF graph options
Range
The line: the MUF for a 3000 km hop measured by the Juliusruh ionosonde (MHz, left scale). The bars: the reporters of each logged transmission (right scale). Tap the chart for the values.
Replay options
Date (UTC)
Speed
The snapshots stored by the logger (about one every 10 minutes): the reporters of that moment and the beacon's position then. The arrows step a day with data. Analysis, History and WPRS open Replay at a chosen moment.
History options
One UTC day: the positions on the map (green the first, blue the last) and every logged transmission, newest first. Tap a row to open that moment in Replay.
Statistics options
Range
The bars: the farthest station of each day (km). The line: the most reporters of one transmission that day.
Analysis options
Period
The full page's Propagation Analysis, one card under the other. The period is also at the top of the view. How to read each chart: the WSPR Logger User Guide (General → User guides). ▶ in the tables opens that moment in Replay.
WPRS options
List
Days with movement or distance from home of at least
Track of
Speed
Whisper Position Reporting System: the beacon's position history. The squares the car was in (darker: more fixes), the path, start (green) and end (red), the home square (red ring). Tap a day or a trip to show it; ▶ plays the fixes. Replay opens the shown fix in Replay. Remembered on the phone.
General
Theme: the button at the top right — a tap steps Dark (☾) → Light (☀) → Auto (◐, follows the phone's setting); the same setting as the full page. The dot at the top right: the beacon — green heard in the last 20 minutes, amber within 2 hours, red older.

About
Operator
Otso Laakso / OH2GAX
Transmitter
WSPR transmitter based on the ZachTek desktop transmitter, 200 mW (23 dBm) transmit power
Availability
Data is available only while the WSPR transmitter in the car is running. It is powered by 30 000 mAh batteries, so it can sometimes be off. Typically the service runs 24/7 during the summer season (March–November) and is off during the winter season.
Antenna
Diamond monoband antenna for 14 MHz (20 m), mounted on a VW Touran car
Home locator
KP20JG
The actual location changes as the car moves — the map and the Locator always show the latest reported position.
