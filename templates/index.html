Every transmission carries the locator the transmitter computed from its GPS fix, so the logged spots form a track with the resolution of one Maidenhead subsquare (about 4.6 km). Moved = largest distance between two squares used that day; away = farthest distance from the home square (the most frequent locator), which also catches days parked far from home. Days are UTC days. A trip is a run of fixes more than 25 km from home that lasts until the car is back near home (silent night-time gaps of up to 36 h do not end it); its path includes the legs out and back.
