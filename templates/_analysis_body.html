A “snapshot” is one poll cycle (about every 10 minutes); its reporter count is the number of different stations heard in the preceding 60 minutes. MUF and reporter counts both follow the day/night cycle, so the raw correlation overstates the link; the bar charts therefore compare each snapshot with the typical value for its UTC hour. MUF is measured at the Juliusruh ionosonde in northern Germany, which only roughly represents your paths. Snapshots where the solar feed returned 0 are ignored for that chart. All figures are computed from the local replay database; nothing is queried from wspr.live.
